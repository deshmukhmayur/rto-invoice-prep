## The workflow

<Steps>
1. **Upload** — click *Upload invoices* or drop PDFs anywhere on the
   window. Load one invoice or a whole day's batch.
2. **Verify** — click a file in the list to open its card. Check the
   invoice details and the *Final Invoice Totals* panel against your own
   copy of the invoice.
3. **Export** — pick the output folder, adjust the naming pattern if you
   like, and click *Export* (or *Export as Zip* for a single archive).
</Steps>

The whole loop takes a couple of minutes for a batch. There are no settings
to configure and no data is kept between sessions — every batch starts from
a clean slate.

<Aside type="note">
  RTO Invoice Prep works on the **OEM tax invoice** (the "Tax Invoice"
  printed by the manufacturer's system), not on the purchase order, the
  delivery note, or your dealership's own challan.
</Aside>

Every PDF you upload becomes a row in the list on the left, with a status
dot:

- a **green dot** means the invoice parsed and was corrected — ready to
  export;
- an **amber dot** means the row needs attention: the parser refused the
  file, or the corrected copy could not be written;
- while files are being read, a progress bar shows *Reading N of M…*.

Rows you no longer want can be removed with the ✕ button; removing a row
never touches any file on disk.

Click a row to open its card: the invoice number, date, customer, model,
chassis and engine numbers as the app read them, and the totals panel.

## What the app removes

OEM tax invoices carry a line such as:

> Less: Dealer Discount ₹ 18,500

That discount was already settled between you and the OEM. When the RTO
office sees it on the registration copy, they ask for paperwork you don't
have — so the line has to come off. RTO Invoice Prep removes:

- the **discount amount** from the discount line,
- the discount's share of **taxable value**, CGST, and SGST,
- stale **totals** and **rounding** figures, and
- the **amount in words**, rewritten for the new total.

Everything else on the invoice — chassis number, engine number, vehicle
description, HSN, dealer details, taxes actually collected — stays exactly
as the OEM printed it.

Each file's card shows a *Final Invoice Totals* panel — a facsimile of the
invoice's own totals block. Every recalculated figure appears twice: the
original value struck through, the corrected value beside it in a
highlight. Deduction rows that appear between *Ex Showroom Price* and *Net
Amount* — a **PM E-DRIVE Subsidy** on EV invoices, for example — are
carried over unchanged: they are government credits, not the dealer's
discount.

The panel is read-only: the discount comes off automatically, and the
amounts are recomputed from the figures the app read. If a struck-through
value doesn't match your own copy of the invoice, trust your copy — remove
the file and check the PDF you were given.

## What the app recalculates

RTO Invoice Prep recomputes the money figures from the remaining taxable
value using the GST rates shown on the invoice itself:

- **Total taxable value** = price − discount.
- **CGST / SGST** = taxable value × the rate printed on your invoice
  (typically 14% + 14% for most two-wheelers, or 18% split evenly for
  standard vehicles).
- **Net amount** = ex-showroom price − deduction rows.
- **Grand total** = net amount, **rounded** to the nearest rupee, with the
  round-off shown separately.
- **Amount in words** = the rounded grand total, spelled out in Indian
  numbering (e.g. "Rupees One Lakh Twelve Thousand Three Hundred Forty Five
  Only").

If the invoice shows a different GST structure, the app still uses the
rates **printed on that invoice**, so the corrected copy always matches the
tax your OEM actually collected.

## Exporting the corrected files

The export bar at the bottom of the window controls where corrected copies
go and what they are called:

- **Save to** — the output folder. The default is your Downloads folder;
  click the button to pick another.
- **Naming** — a file name pattern. `{name}` is the original file name,
  `{seq}` is the file's position in the batch, and `{date}` is today's
  date. The default pattern `{name}-RTO` turns `invoice.pdf` into:

  ```
  invoice.pdf       →  invoice-RTO.pdf
  ```

- **Export N files** writes every ready row to the output folder at once.
  The ▾ caret next to it offers **Export as Zip**, which packs the same
  files into a single `rto-invoices-YYYY-MM-DD.zip` archive.
- Rows that could not be written get a **Retry this file** button; rows the
  parser refused never export.

<Aside type="caution">
  The app refuses to overwrite your original file. Exported copies get a
  distinct name, and if that name collides with an existing file, the app
  writes a numbered copy instead.
</Aside>

Some invoices are refused rather than corrected — the row shows a message
beginning **Template validation failed**. Common reasons:

- **"No Discount row found"** — the PDF is not the OEM tax invoice (it
  may be the purchase order or delivery note), or the discount is
  genuinely zero. If the discount is zero, hand over the original.
- **"Multiple Discount rows found"** — the invoice has more than one
  discount line, which this tool does not attempt.
- **"expected 2 pages, found N"** or a label out of position — the OEM's
  layout has drifted from the template the tool was validated against.
- **"Discount is 0.00 — nothing to remove"** — nothing to do; the
  original already adds up.
- **"Not a readable PDF document"** — the file is damaged, encrypted, or
  not a PDF at all.

A refused row exports nothing. The app never guesses at an invoice it
doesn't recognise — a wrong guess on an RTO copy costs more than a refusal.

If your layout really is different, [file a bug](faq/#reporting-bugs)
with a redacted copy of the invoice.

## Troubleshooting

### A row says the write failed

The output folder may be read-only, full, or briefly locked by another
program. Pick another folder, or click **Retry this file** once the
problem is fixed.

### The parser crashed on this file

A rare bug in the app itself, not a problem with your invoice. Use the
**Open Log** button on the crash panel, then report it via
[the FAQ](faq/#reporting-bugs) with the log attached.

### The amounts don't match my invoice

Re-upload the PDF and check the discount amount digit by digit, then check
the GST rates printed on the invoice. If your invoice uses a structure the
app doesn't understand, treat the output as a starting point and verify by
hand before submission.

### The app won't start

- Make sure you installed the latest release from the
  [Releases page](https://github.com/deshmukhmayur/rto-invoice-prep/releases).
- If Windows SmartScreen blocks the first run, click **More info** →
  **Run anyway**.
- If it still won't start, [report the bug](faq/#reporting-bugs) with your
  Windows version and the release you downloaded.
