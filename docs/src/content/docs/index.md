Free Windows app for dealership desks that handle vehicle registration.
Open the tax invoice PDF your OEM system printed, let the app remove the
discount line, and it rewrites the GST breakdown and the amount in words so
the RTO copy adds up.

- **Local first.** Invoices never leave your computer. The app talks to
  the internet only to check for updates.
- **Two minutes per invoice.** Drop the PDF, save the corrected copy
  alongside your originals.
- **Made for a single dealership desk.** No accounts, no servers, no
  subscriptions.

<Aside type="tip">
  The app reads OEM tax-invoice PDFs of the standard template used by
  motorcycle makers. If your invoice layout is different, see the
  [FAQ](/faq/).
</Aside>
