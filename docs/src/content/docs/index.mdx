<div class="home-intro">
Free Windows app for dealership desks that handle vehicle registration.
Open the tax invoice PDF your OEM system printed, let the app strike the
discount line, and it rewrites the GST breakdown and the amount in words
so the RTO copy adds up.
</div>

<section class="home-section" aria-labelledby="how-it-works">
  <span class="home-eyebrow" id="how-it-works">On the corrected copy</span>
  <h2>What changes — and what doesn’t</h2>
  <ul class="home-points">
    <li>
      <span class="point-label point-amber">struck</span>
      <span>**Less: Dealer Discount** — gone, along with its share of taxable
      value, CGST, and SGST.</span>
    </li>
    <li>
      <span class="point-label">rewritten</span>
      <span>**Totals and round-off**, recomputed at the GST rates printed on
      your own invoice.</span>
    </li>
    <li>
      <span class="point-label">rewritten</span>
      <span>**Amount in words**, spelled out in Indian numbering for the new
      grand total.</span>
    </li>
    <li>
      <span class="point-label point-amber">untouched</span>
      <span>**Everything else** — chassis, engine, HSN, dealer details, taxes
      actually collected — exactly as the OEM printed it.</span>
    </li>
  </ul>
</section>

<section class="home-section" aria-labelledby="why-local">
  <span class="home-eyebrow" id="why-local">On your desk</span>
  <h2>Made for a single dealership desk</h2>
  <ul class="home-points">
    <li>
      <span class="point-label">local</span>
      <span>**Local first.** Invoices never leave your computer. The app talks
      to the internet only to check for updates.</span>
    </li>
    <li>
      <span class="point-label">2 min</span>
      <span>**Two minutes per invoice.** Drop the PDF, export the corrected
      copy to your chosen folder.</span>
    </li>
    <li>
      <span class="point-label">0 setup</span>
      <span>**No accounts, no servers, no subscriptions.** Nothing is stored
      between sessions.</span>
    </li>
  </ul>
</section>
