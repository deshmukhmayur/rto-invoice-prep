# Context

Glossary for the RTO invoice fixer. No implementation details — terms and their meaning only.

## Terms

- **OEM invoice**: the PDF generated by the manufacturer's portal. Carries the dealership's discount applied to the vehicle's price.
- **Discount**: a dealership offer, applied as a line item deducted from the vehicle's subtotal. Commercially real, but it lowers the *baseline price* shown on the OEM invoice — which RTO's process does not accommodate.
- **Taxable value**: vehicle subtotal minus the discount. CGST and SGST are calculated on this.
- **Ex-showroom price**: taxable value plus CGST plus SGST; the invoice's net amount before any post-tax deductions.
- **Deduction row**: a line item between Ex Showroom Price and Net Amount that reduces the customer's payable amount after tax — e.g. a **PM E-DRIVE Subsidy** on EV invoices. Government credits, unlike the dealer's discount: they stay on the RTO invoice unchanged, with Net = Ex Showroom − deductions.
- **Grand total**: net amount (ex-showroom price minus deduction rows) plus round-off.
- **RTO invoice**: the full-price invoice (discount removed, all downstream figures recalculated) that RTO accepts for registration and IDV. The accepted baseline is the dealership's long-standing manual edit of the OEM invoice.
- **Official baseline price**: the manufacturer's ex-showroom price, undiscounted. Road tax is a fixed percentage of it, so RTO requires it for registration, IDV, and uniform tax slabs. Legitimate discounts apply to the customer's final on-road bill, not to this baseline.
- **Template variant**: an OEM invoice whose totals block or layout differs from the invoice the tool was validated against — extra line items outside the Ex Showroom Price → Net Amount gap, more than one discount row, a missing discount, different page count, or restyled fields. Variants are outside the tool's validated scope and are refused, never guessed at. Deduction rows (e.g. PM E-DRIVE Subsidy) in that gap are validated, not variants. The tax rate is derived from the invoice itself (CGST ÷ taxable value) rather than assumed, so ICE (9%) and EV (2.5%) invoices are both in scope.
